## 1. Statutory Context: Transition to BSA 2023

In July 2024, the **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)** superseded the historical **Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (IEA)**. 
- The former **Section 65B** of the IEA (Admissibility of Electronic Records) was restructured and modernized under **Section 63 of the BSA, 2023**.
- Section 63 governs the admissibility and production of electronic records, computer outputs, and automated digital captures in legal proceedings.

---

## 2. Core Legal Reality & Honesty Boundary

> **CRITICAL LEGAL DISTINCTION**:
> **Software can generate cryptographically verifiable electronic evidence; software CANNOT certify or guarantee court admissibility.**

Under Section 63(4) of the BSA, 2023:
1. **Admissibility is a Judicial Determination**: Admissibility of an electronic record is decided exclusively by the presiding judicial magistrate or judge, based on the totality of forensic custody, relevance, and the credible testimony/certificate of a qualified person.
2. **Role of IBVAP**: IBVAP is an **integrity assurance and chain-of-custody engine**. It generates an unalterable technical audit trail:
   - What image pixels were ingested (`input_hash`).
   - What model weights were loaded (`model_digest`).
   - What hyperparameters were active (`config_hash`).
   - What detections were produced (`output_hash`).
   - In what temporal sequence they occurred (`sequence_id`, `timestamp`, `nonce`, `previous_record_hash`).

### Grounded Statutory Statement:
> **"IBVAP generates a cryptographically verifiable electronic-evidence certificate designed to support evidence/audit workflows under applicable provisions of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023."**

### Prescribed Jury Talking Point:
> **"We generate technically verifiable evidence. Whether that evidence is legally admissible is determined under the applicable law and judicial process."**
